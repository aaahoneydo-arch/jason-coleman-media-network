Return and Refund Policy

Last Updated: October 11, 2026

Thank you for shopping at Jason's Marketplace (jasontvmarketplace.com). We take pride in delivering top-quality custom apparel, printed merchandise, gamer gear, and collectibles. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, we are here to help.

Quick Summary:

Return Window

30 days from the date your package is delivered.

Eligible Items

Both defective and non-defective items accepted.

Restocking Fee

$0.00 (Zero restocking fee charged).

Refund Method

100% refund credited back to original payment method.

1. 30-Day Return Window

You have 30 days from the date your tracking confirms delivery to initiate a return or exchange request. To be eligible for a standard return, items must be in the original condition in which they were received (unwashed, unworn, and with original tags/packaging where applicable).

2. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If you receive an item that is misprinted, damaged during transit, defective, or incorrect:

  • Contact us within 30 days at aaahoneydo@gmail.com or our customer care team with your Order ID and photos showing the issue.
  • We will provide an immediate free replacement or a full refund with zero return shipping charges to you.

3. Non-Defective Returns & Buyer Remorse

If you changed your mind, selected the wrong size, or want a different color/design:

  • We gladly accept returns and exchanges on non-defective items within the 30-day window.
  • We never charge a restocking fee.
  • Customers may send the product back using tracked mail to our return facility.

4. Exchanges

Yes, we accept product exchanges! If you need a different size, fit, or variant, reach out to us and we will promptly coordinate an exchange so you get the exact gear you want.

5. How to Initiate a Return

  1. Email us at aaahoneydo@gmail.com with your order confirmation number and reason for return.
  2. Our customer support team will respond within 24 hours with return instructions and the return authorization.
  3. Pack the item securely and ship it back using the provided return address.

6. Refund Processing Time

Once your return is received and inspected at our distribution facility, we will notify you via email. Approved refunds are processed immediately and automatically applied to your original method of payment (credit card, debit card, or digital wallet) within 3 to 5 business days, depending on your card issuer.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions about our Return and Refund Policy or need assistance with an existing order, please contact us:

Store: Jason's Marketplace
Website: https://jasontvmarketplace.com
Email Support: aaahoneydo@gmail.com
Customer Service Hours: Monday – Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM EST
