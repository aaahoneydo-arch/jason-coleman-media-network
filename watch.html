Stories, books & narrated graphic novels

For illustrated fiction, begin with DRAEVAN Volume One. It introduces a vampire hiding in modern Miami and the journalist who discovers his secrets. Then try Dark And The Light Legions, another fully narrated graphic novel.

Prefer a short introduction? Watch The Real Americana trailer, TIFF, or Conscious. The Books playlist brings these videos together; the reading guide connects them to Jason’s Amazon books.

History, cinematic storytelling & space

For a character-centered fictional short, watch A Day in the Life of a Samurai’s Daughter. For history, choose Alexander the Great: The Man, The Myth, The LEGEND or SPARTA: The Brutal Truth Behind the Legend.

Interested in the cosmos? Try The Most Amazing Facts About Space or When Worlds Collide! For longer listening, browse Sleep Time: Calm Facts and Stories.

Original JasonTV songs

Listen to Can’t Turn It Off with its fashion montage, or switch to Never Quit. For country party energy, try Git Down With the Git Down. For creator humor, there’s The Algorithm Hates Me.

Comedy & gaming

Take a break with Try Not To Laugh Challenge IMPOSSIBLE. For classic television, explore the Beverly Hillbillies playlist. For gameplay, open JasonTV’s Gaming playlist.
