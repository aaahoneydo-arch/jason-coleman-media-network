Military Documentary Economics: How I Earn $3K/Month Without Ads

Posted September 27, 2026 | Creator Economics Series

In 2019 I wasn't making money from my 800+ military history videos. Today I pull $3,000+ monthly from a single YouTube channel - no sponsorships, no products, no ads in videos.

The Revenue System I Built

YouTube Ad Revenue: $1,200/month

800+ military documentaries. No sponsorships. No products. No affiliate links in videos. Just consistent, quality content with $4-7 CPM.

Book Sales (Cross-Promotion): $1,400/month

My novel "The Shattered Crown" referenced in every military video. Amazon affiliate links in descriptions. Kindle Unlimited + paperback + audiobook.

Game Sales: $400/month

"SO IT'S WAR THEN" tactical RTS game. $19.99 price point, 500+ monthly sales. Early access model.

The Content Loop

  1. Monday/Tuesday: Research military topic + film 1-2 documentary segments
  2. Wednesday/Thursday: Edit footage + write book chapter
  3. Friday: Upload video with cross-promo links
  4. Saturday/Sunday: Community engagement + plan next week

Why This Works

Put all content in one place. Military historians love deep technical dives. One video leads to another. They binge-watch the whole channel.

Key Metrics I Track

  • Average view duration: 8+ minutes
  • Click-through rate: 9% on end screens
  • Playlist completion: 67%
  • Watch time: >50% at 10 minutes
