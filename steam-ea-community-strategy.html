Steam Early Access Publishing Strategy 2024

Jason Coleman's proven framework from 12 published EA titles

Store Page Pre-Launch

  • Development Timeline GIF - 1080p animated roadmap showing progress
  • Screenshot Chronology - Early mockup to polished build (not just final art)
  • Community Discord Integration - Auto-notify subscribed players of major updates

Irregular Release Cadence

Never announce exact dates. Use "Upcoming Next" windows. Players who can't check daily still feel involved.

Revenue Threshold Detection

Monitor SteamDB for competitor traffic drops. When your niche genre spikes, accelerate your EA timeline.

Key Metrics to Track

  • Player Hours vs. Update Frequency ratio
  • Community Post Engagement Quality (not just quantity)
  • Steam Review Sentiment Analysis (positive/negative/technical)
