# RSS Feed Submission Package for Multi-Platform Distribution

**Primary RSS Feed:** https://jasontvmarketplace.com/feed.xml

## RSS Feed Aggregator Services (Free)

### Medium Import via RSS
- Medium RSS importer accepts feeds automatically
- Submit: https://medium.com/me/feed
- Content appears as drip-fed posts
- Tags pulled from content

### LinkedIn RSS Integration
- LinkedIn accepts RSS feeds through third-party tools
- Recommended: Make.com, Zapier, or SocialChamp
- URL: https://www.make.com/en/integrations/linkedin/rss
- Cost: Free tier available

### Reddit RSS Auto-Posting
- Reddit supports RSS via AutoModerator
- Setup: Configure automoderator in subreddit
- Or use: https://ifttt.com/applets/G7jpfrvD-share-new-rss-feed-items-to-linkedin
- Manual subreddit: r/autoModerator

### Twitter/X RSS Threads
- Twitter doesn't natively support RSS
- Use: https://www.pabbly.com/how-to-create-posts-in-medium-from-rss-feed-items-using-pabbly-connect/
- Or: https://socialrise.com/tools/rss-to-twitter
