A Beautiful Alibi

The Borrowed Names, Book One — Near-Future Espionage, Identity & Deception

In near-future 2041, eighteen-year-old Florida diner worker Mara Elise Voss—who learned Russian from her grandmother and knows how to fix broken machines—is framed in a staged arrest and recruited into an overseas intelligence fellowship in wintry Saint Petersburg. Caught between CIA handler Evelyn Shaw, surveillance heir Aleksandr Orlov, and a vanished operative named Nika who planted the bait, Mara must uncover how Meridian Civic Systems engineers digital provenance before she becomes someone else's alibi.
