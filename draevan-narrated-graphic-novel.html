Ancient secrets beneath modern Miami

Draevan has lived for 4,000 years. In Miami, his wealth and charm conceal a past that journalist Kira Morrison never expected to uncover. When she takes the wrong elevator beneath his laboratory, her investigation enters a hidden world of immortals, dangerous alliances, and buried memories.

This video adapts the opening arc of Jason Coleman’s novel into 116 illustrated story pages with character narration. Volume One is an introduction to the story, rather than a reading of the entire prose novel. It combines supernatural mystery, urban fantasy, and slow-burn romance.

About this adaptation

Story and original novel by Jason Coleman. The graphic adaptation uses generative AI illustrations under the author’s creative direction, synthetic character narration with Kokoro TTS, and video assembly in CapCut.

Intended for mature audiences: fantasy violence, strong language, and non-explicit romance.
