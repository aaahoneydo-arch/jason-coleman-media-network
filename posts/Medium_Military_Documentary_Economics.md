# Military Documentary Economics: How I Earn $3K/month Without Ads

In 2019 I wasn't making money from my 800+ military history videos. Today I pull $3,000+ monthly from a single YouTube channel - no sponsorships, no ads, no excuses.

## The Revenue System I Built

**YouTube Ad Revenue: $1,200/month**
- CPM of $5-7 from documentary content
- 600,000 monthly views across 800+ videos
- Consistent Tuesday/Thursday upload schedule

**Book Sales (Cross-Promotion): $1,400/month**
- My novel "The Shattered Crown" referenced in every military video
- Amazon affiliate links in video descriptions
- KDP Select expanded distribution

**Game Sales: $400/month**
- "SO IT'S WAR THEN" mentioned in tank/war tactics videos
- Steam store links in pinned comments
- Early access community cross-pollination

## The Content Loop

1. Research military topic (1 week)
2. Film documentary (2 days)
3. Edit with book/game references (3 days)
4. Upload with cross-promo links (1 day)
5. Engage with comments (daily)

## Key Metrics I Track

- Average view duration: 8+ minutes
- Click-through rate: 9% on end screens
- Playlist completion: 67%
- Video retention: >50% at 10 minutes

This system works because I solved the "why should they watch" question in every video description.
