Title: "From $0 Creative Income to $10K/Month: The Multi-Platform System"

From military veteran to creator entrepreneur building sustainable income across 6 platforms

In 2018 I had zero creative income. Today I earn $8,000-12,000 monthly from content I create. Here's the actual system—not theory.

## The 6 Income Streams

### 1. YouTube Ad Revenue (35% of income)
800+ military history documentaries. No sponsorships. No products. Just consistent, quality content.
Key insight: Upload schedule matters more than video quality. 2x weekly at 3pm EST.

### 2. Amazon Book Sales (25% of income)
9 published novels. Kindle Unlimited + paperback + audiobook.
Cross-promotion in documentary descriptions and end screens.

### 3. TikTok Creator Fund (15% of income)
Daily comedy shorts targeting blue-collar workers.
Volume over virality - need 5M+ monthly views for meaningful payouts.

### 4. Steam Game Sales (15% of income)
SO IT'S WAR THEN - tactical RTS game. Early access model.
Documentaries feature game concepts, driving wishlists.

### 5. Affiliate Marketing (7% of income)
Equipment recommendations in documentary production videos.
Trust-based; only recommend gear I actually use.

### 6. Merchandise (3% of income)
Print-on-demand t-shirts, stickers, art prints.
Simple fulfillment through Teespring and similar platforms.

## Why This System Survives Platform Changes

When TikTok changed algorithm in 2023: Book sales covered 4-month gap

When YouTube demonetized 200 videos: Games + affiliate grew 35%

When Amazon changed KDP rules: Started direct audiobook sales

Never rely on one platform. Build systems, not viral moments.

## The Consistency Factor

What separates successful creators from dreamers?

They treat creativity like a business. Schedule content, batch production, analyze metrics.

I film 4 documentaries weekly. Write 2,000 words daily. Edit 3 videos weekend.

Consistency compounds. Systems scale. Platforms fail. Your audience remains.
