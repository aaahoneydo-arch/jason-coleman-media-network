Where to start with Jason Coleman’s fiction

For fantasy, The Shattered Crown follows Kaia Blackwood and the broken Crown Shards. A Beautiful Alibi opens Mara Voss’s espionage story. For post-apocalyptic fiction, start Dixon’s Journal with A House Beneath the Ash. Science fiction, vampires and graphic novels

Reading after the ashfall or entering the espionage series

In Dixon’s Journal, a Yellowstone eruption turns an ordinary family’s basement into shelter. A House Beneath the Ash starts the survival story. A Beautiful Alibi follows Mara Voss from a fabricated drug case into an intelligence assignment.
