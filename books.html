Books by Jason Coleman

Choose your next read by the kind of story or question that interests you. These nine titles appear in Jason Coleman’s Amazon catalog.

Start with a video

DRAEVAN’s narrated graphic novel introduces the opening arc of the vampire story. For a brief look at other books, watch The Real Americana, TIFF, or Conscious.

Choose by interest

Fantasy and supernatural fiction: The Shattered Crown, DRAEVAN, and The Moonlight Outcasts. Speculative fiction: TIFF and Life In The Year 2035. A novel inspired by true events: The Real Americana. Ideas, politics, and history: How Matter Became Conscious, The Deception Habit, and Old Men Start Wars That Young Men Die In.

These are choices by interest, not a shared series reading order. DRAEVAN and TIFF each identify themselves as Book One.

The Shattered Crown

Protagonist: Kaia Blackwood. In the volcanic shadow of the Cinderpeak Mountains, a blacksmith's daughter discovers that the sundering of ancient Crown Shards has unleashed waking wraith legions and the immortal Lich King. Real steel, no prophecies.

The Real Americana

Some places break you. Some teach you how to stay. From the rugged hills of Kentucky through addiction, jail, fatherhood, loss, and the long road toward recovery. A gripping story about second chances and finding home.

Draevan : Book One: The Immortal

A 4,000-year-old vampire living as a modern Miami billionaire. When journalist Kira Morrison stumbles into his subterranean lab, ancient immortals clash against modern biotech conspiracies.

The Deception Habit

How Matter Became Conscious
