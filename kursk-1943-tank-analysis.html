Eastern Front Tank Battles: Kursk 1943 Tactical Analysis

From Jason Coleman's 800+ military documentary collection

The Battle of Kursk represents the largest armored engagement in history. This analysis examines why Soviet deep battle doctrine succeeded against German offensive strategy.

Key Tactical Innovations

  1. Deep Infiltration - Soviets bypassed strongpoints instead of frontal assaults
  2. Anti-Tank Artillery Placement
  3. T-34 Reliability vs Panzer IV Maintenance - Harsh Russian winter favored Soviet production model
