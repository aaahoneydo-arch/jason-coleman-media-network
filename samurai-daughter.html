A life shaped by duty. A choice to leave.

This narrated short film follows a young woman whose daily life is governed by the expectations of a samurai household. She observes the boys training in the courtyard, walks through carefully ordered gardens, and writes letters she cannot send. Those quiet routines reveal a growing wish to choose her own future.

A meeting with a ronin gives that wish a human connection. The story moves from observation to a decision: will she accept the life chosen for her, or step beyond the household that defines her?

What to watch for

Notice the contrast between the public image of her father as a warrior and the way she experiences his authority at home. The courtyard, paper screen, letters, and garden make her restrictions visible without relying on a battlefield. The central conflict is personal freedom against inherited duty.
