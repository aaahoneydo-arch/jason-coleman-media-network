YouTube Algorithm 2024: Shadow Ban Detection Methods

Jason Coleman's channel analytics from 800+ military documentary videos

Key Warning Signs

  • Traffic Source Drop - Search traffic falls 40%+ while suggested stays same
  • Impressions vs Clicks - High impressions, low click-through rate
  • Audience Retention - Normal retention but low completion rate

Recovery Protocol

  1. Delete and re-upload problematic video
  2. Change title/keywords minimally
  3. Create community post explaining context
